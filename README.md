# I Wish

[English](README.md) | [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

Wish is the most powerful magic mortals can wield.
Speak your wish aloud, and the world gives it shape.

Turn a substantial product, feature, or subsystem into explicit success criteria,
an evidenced solution, and a verified result. Use I Wish when product,
architecture, or reuse choices remain open or delegated, even when the desired
behavior is clear. See [SKILL.md](SKILL.md).

Invoke `$i-wish` with the desired outcome and known constraints. The workflow
defines acceptance and scope, researches triggered choices before deciding,
builds and verifies coherent slices, and preserves decisions and current docs.
Dependent implementation requires the brief, applicable research evidence,
resolved user decisions, and a slice verification plan.

Evidence gates are required; repeated approvals are not. Carry existing
authorization forward, ask about unresolved choices affecting scope, cost, data
exposure, or hard-to-reverse commitments, and honor requested staged approvals.

Runtime guidance: [shaping](references/shaping.md),
[research](references/research.md), [delivery](references/delivery.md),
[documentation](references/documentation.md), and
[games](references/game-projects.md). Read references at their specified triggers.
See [host integration](references/host-adapters.md) for installation and adapter
behavior, and [behavioral cases](evals/cases.md) for development checks. Separate
copies and host installations are not automatically synchronized.
