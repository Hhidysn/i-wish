# Speak in effects

Optional examples when explaining a consequential choice. Match the user's
register per decision; do not assign a permanent expertise level.

Explain: what they see → how they use it → cost/limits → decision needed.
Translate a necessary term once; keep a shared name. State verified, inferred or
unverified facts and the check that settles uncertainty.

| Mechanism question | Useful decision |
| --- | --- |
| Database? | Must records survive closing; who needs access? |
| Cache? | Must the next opening be faster, or always current? |
| Local or cloud? | Which devices/users need the data; what may leave the device? |

Example: “关闭再打开，记录仍在这台电脑上。无需设置。没有月费；换电脑
需要手动导出。以后需要多设备共享时，再确认同步方案。”

For developers, explain relevant contracts, alternatives, failure modes, operations
and maintenance. For learners, explain the consequence and revisit condition.
Ask outcome choices they own; investigate discoverable project facts yourself.
Recommendations need defaults and concrete consequences, not unexplained tool names.

Shorten action clauses; remove repetition. Keep condition, actor, object,
authorization and pass criterion. Do not compress a real decision into vague keywords.
