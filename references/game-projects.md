# Games

Read before shaping a game; apply the selected mode and shared role contracts.
Do not turn a demo into a whole engine or reduce an approved framework to one scene.

Ask about the playable loop, audience, player feedback, content scale, target
hardware/input, offline/network requirements and save compatibility. Define an
observable play scenario and relevant failure/recovery, not only a subsystem list.

Inspect existing scenes, assets, interfaces and saves as reuse/repair/replace
candidates. A clean replacement need not retain prototype contracts; deletion and
irreversible save migration still follow authorization. Prefer engine/platform
features, compatible maintained plugins and licensed assets to custom infrastructure.

Research authority/networking, persistence, streaming, unusual simulation, export
support and performance assumptions before dependent design. Check asset scale,
rigs/render pipeline, target budget and redistribution terms before adoption.
Full: cover player/world/rules/content/AI/persistence/presentation ownership and
shared contracts; detail individual modules through the readiness loop.

Independent tests derive rule/state expectations from approved play behavior.
Fresh UX receives player goal, ordinary controls/help and isolated save state;
observe actual input, camera, feedback, failure and recovery in the running game.
Verify save/load, network authority, performance and platform export where in scope.
Record unavailable hardware/platform checks as unverified.

Design-only: review coverage and specify future play/technical checks; do not claim
runtime or player experience has been tested. Agent observations do not replace
agreed human judgment of enjoyment or feel.
