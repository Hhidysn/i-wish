# Interview guide

Read when material product choices need clarification. Inspect discoverable
facts first. Focus on observable experience, audience, scope, constraints, and
acceptance. Ask questions whose answers change the result; batch independent
questions when helpful and follow the host question interface. Give concrete
recommendations and trade-offs without fixed question or round quotas.

When the user also explicitly requests Grilling, follow its one-question-at-a-time
interview and wait for shared-understanding confirmation before implementation.
That requested gate takes precedence over this guide's default batching and flow.

Use the user's vocabulary. Explain outcomes to nontechnical users instead of
requiring technology choices; respect technical constraints they supply.
Adopt reasonable reversible defaults for delegated choices such as "都可以".
Leave unresolved consequential scope, spending, data, or commitment decisions
for the user. A correction is not approval of unrelated decisions.

Once scope is coherent, summarize goals, constraints, assumptions, and acceptance
criteria. Continue authorized research and implementation without a ritual
approval of the summary. In explicitly gated mode, obtain Gate 1 approval if it
has not already been given and honor any stricter boundary the user requested.
Silence is not approval.
